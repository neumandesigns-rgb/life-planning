### Jewelry Store — The Gold Chain

**Where to go (open by 9 AM, no waiting):**
- Kohl's Fine Jewelry
- JCPenney Fine Jewelry
- Walmart Fine Jewelry

**If you don't mind waiting until 10 AM:**
- Kay Jewelers (entry-level collection)
- Zales

**What to ask for:** "A 10K or 14K solid gold chain"
- Rope chain or box chain are the most versatile styles
- Bring the necklace pendant so you can match the clasp and length
- **Avoid** anything labeled "gold-tone," "gold-plated," or "gold-filled" — these tarnish
- **Budget:** $40–$150 gets you solid quality at these stores

## Cabin Meal Plan

**Dinner (Tonight)**
Grilled salmon in foil packets: lay salmon on foil, top with butter, garlic, lemon slices, salt and pepper. Fold into a sealed packet and cook on the propane grill ~12–15 min. Do the same with the veggies. Pop the sparkling cider and make a toast.

**Snack Night**
Cheese and crackers, dark chocolate, popcorn — put on a game or a movie.

**Breakfast (Tomorrow Morning)**
- *Overnight oats:* assemble in mason jars tonight before bed — zero effort in the morning
- *Cast iron pancakes:* cook on the propane grill on the cast iron skillet — a fun little experience
