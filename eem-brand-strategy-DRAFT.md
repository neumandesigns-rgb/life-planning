## Section 01 — Identity Statement

Eastern European Ministries is a Bible distribution organization built on a simple and non-negotiable conviction: every person deserves access to God's Word, no matter the cost or the distance required to deliver it. For more than 60 years, EEM has published and distributed Scripture across Eastern Europe and the surrounding regions, through a network of thousands of trusted local partners who place the Bible directly into the hands of people who have asked for it. The work is real, the need is current, and the requests keep coming.

What defines EEM is not distribution. It is response. There is a critical difference between an organization that sends Bibles into the world and one that receives requests from people who are already reaching for them. EEM operates on the second model. These are not Bibles waiting on people. These are people waiting on Bibles. When someone in Ukraine, Kazakhstan, North Macedonia, or Iran asks for Scripture in the language of their heart, EEM exists so that the answer can simply be yes. The work is demand-driven by design, which means there is no guessing, no shelf inventory, no supply in search of a use. There is a person, a request, and a response.

The theological conviction underneath all of this is not complicated, but it is total: people were made to know God, and Scripture is how He reveals Himself. The Bible is not a book EEM believes in sentimentally or distributes symbolically. It is, as EEM understands it, the living and active Word of God, the means through which He draws people to Christ, forms faith, strengthens the church, and invites human beings into the life they were created for. Without Scripture, people remain cut off from the truth about who God is and what He has done through Jesus. With it, everything becomes possible. EEM places the Bible in someone's hands and trusts God to do what only He can do from there.

EEM is not the source of this work, and it does not claim to be. God is pursuing people across nations and languages, and EEM is a channel through which that pursuit moves. Your dollar does not just go further with EEM; it gets all the way to the moment it was intended for. The role of this organization is to remove barriers, cost, language, logistics, supply, and to ensure that when someone is ready for the Word of God, nothing stands between that person and the Bible. EEM is most faithful when it is almost invisible: the Scripture is in someone's hands, the local church is doing its work, and EEM was simply the means that made the moment possible.

That posture shapes everything about how EEM operates and communicates. EEM works across Protestant, Orthodox, and Catholic traditions, because its loyalty is to Scripture, not to any denominational tribe or institutional identity. It is humble, steady, and not easily drawn toward noise or self-promotion. It moves toward the overlooked rather than the prominent. It does not overclaim its role or compete with the partners it serves. What would make EEM angry is anything that keeps people from the Word of God when the barrier could be removed. What would make EEM cry is seeing someone hold a Bible in their own language for the very first time, and knowing what that moment means. That is the ground beneath everything. That is what EEM is.
