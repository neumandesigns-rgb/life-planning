## Section 01 — Identity Statement

Eastern European Ministries exists because people across Eastern Europe and the surrounding regions are already asking for the Word of God. EEM's model is not distribution in search of recipients; it is response to people who are reaching. These are not Bibles waiting on people. These are people waiting on Bibles. When someone in Ukraine, Kazakhstan, North Macedonia, or Iran requests Scripture in the language of their heart, EEM is the organization that makes the answer yes: publishing, funding, and delivering the Bible through a network of trusted local partners who place it directly in the hands of the person who asked.

The conviction underneath this work is total: people were made to know God, and Scripture is how He reveals Himself. The Bible is not something EEM distributes symbolically or believes in sentimentally. It is, as EEM understands it, the living and active Word of God, the means through which He draws people to Christ, forms faith, and invites human beings into the life they were created for. EEM is not the source of this work. God is pursuing people across nations and languages, and EEM is a channel through which that pursuit moves. The organization is most faithful when it is almost invisible: the Scripture is in someone's hands, the local church is doing its work, and EEM was simply the means that made the moment possible.
